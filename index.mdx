Terminal 1d6 RPG is a fully browser-based, offline-capable role-playing game where every action you take — every attack, spell, or daring escape — is resolved by a single roll of a d6. No sign-up required, no download needed. Open the game, build your character, and let the die decide your legend.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game">
    Launch Terminal 1d6 RPG in any modern browser — no account, no install required to play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Character">
    Answer nine quick questions about your adventurer: their name, class, era, companion, backstory, weapons, strengths, and struggles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Dungeon">
    You are dropped into a procedurally-drawn location with an enemy and a treasure to claim. Type your action and press Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Roll the Die">
    Every action triggers an automatic 1d6 roll. Roll a 6 for a perfect triumph with bonus loot — or a 1 for a dire misfortune and lost HP. Survive as long as you can.
  </Step>
</Steps>
