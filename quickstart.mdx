This guide walks you through everything that happens from the moment you open Terminal 1d6 RPG to the moment you roll your first die. By the end you will have a named character standing in a procedurally generated dungeon location, ready to take on the henchman blocking your path. The whole process takes under five minutes and requires nothing but a browser.

<Note>
  Your session is auto-saved after every action using your browser's
  localStorage. You can close the tab at any point and return to exactly where
  you left off — no manual saving needed.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Game in Your Browser">
    Navigate to the Terminal 1d6 RPG URL in any modern browser. The game runs
    entirely client-side, so there is nothing to install before you can play.

    Supported browsers include:

    - **Chrome** (desktop and Android)
    - **Firefox** (desktop)
    - **Safari** (desktop, iPhone, and iPad)
    - **Edge** (desktop)

    The terminal interface loads immediately. You will see the game title and a
    prompt waiting for you to begin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the Character Creation Wizard">
    Type anything or press **Enter** at the opening prompt to launch the
    9-step character creation wizard. The Dungeon Master asks you one question
    at a time directly in the terminal. Type your answer and press **Enter** (or
    tap **SEND** on mobile) to move to the next question.

    The nine questions, in order, are:

    | # | What the DM Asks | Example Answer |
    |---|-----------------|----------------|
    | 1 | **Your name** | `Sable Vorne` |
    | 2 | **Your class** | `Rogue`, `Chrono-Mage`, `Oathbreaker Paladin` |
    | 3 | **Your timeline or era** | `The Age of Ashen Rust`, `Post-Collapse Year 442` |
    | 4 | **Your familiar or companion** | `A one-eyed raven named Dusk` or `None` |
    | 5 | **Your character bio** | A sentence or two about your background and motivation |
    | 6 | **Your weapons and tools** | `A serrated short sword, lock picks, a smoke canister` |
    | 7 | **Your first strength** | `Shadow movement — you are almost invisible in dim light` |
    | 8 | **Your second strength** | `Quick hands — you can pick a lock in under ten seconds` |
    | 9 | **Your struggles or weaknesses** | `Overconfident in open combat; allergic to arcane residue` |

    After the ninth answer you can add any extra notes for the Dungeon Master —
    special lore, preferred playstyle, or anything else that should colour the
    narration. Press **Enter** on a blank line to skip this step and proceed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the Dungeon">
    Once character creation is complete, the Dungeon Master places you inside
    your first procedurally drawn location. The terminal prints a scene
    description that tells you:

    - **Where you are** — drawn from a pool of 25 unique locations (e.g., a
      collapsed aqueduct, a phosphorescent fungal cavern, a rusted clock tower
      basement)
    - **Who you face** — a henchman enemy drawn from a pool of 25 (e.g., a
      Plague-Masked Enforcer, a Rusted Automaton, a Whisper-Cult Initiate)
    - **What loot is present** — a single item drawn from a pool of 25 (e.g.,
      a Vial of Echo Dust, a Cracked Compass That Points to Regret)

    Read the scene carefully — the environment and enemy description often
    suggest actions that will lead to better roll outcomes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Your First Action">
    Type a free-form action in the input field at the bottom of the terminal and
    press **Enter** or click **SEND**. You are not limited to a menu of
    options — write whatever your character would actually attempt.

    <Tip>
      Not sure what to type for your first action? Try one of these openers:

      - `Attack the henchman with my [weapon]`
      - `Sneak into the shadows and observe before striking`
      - `Cast a spell to disorient the enemy`
      - `Look for an environmental advantage in the room`
      - `Attempt to intimidate the henchman into backing down`

      Descriptive, in-character actions give the Dungeon Master more material
      to work with and often lead to richer narration regardless of the roll.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="See the Dice Roll Result">
    After you submit your action, the game rolls a virtual d6 and displays the
    result directly in the terminal. The outcome is color-coded so you can read
    it at a glance:

    | Roll | Outcome           | Terminal Color  |
    | ---- | ----------------- | --------------- |
    | 6    | Perfect Triumph   | Bright green    |
    | 5    | Strong Success    | Green           |
    | 4    | Partial Success   | Yellow-green    |
    | 3    | Mixed Outcome     | Yellow          |
    | 2    | Setback           | Orange          |
    | 1    | Critical Calamity | Red — and –1 HP |

    Below the roll result, the Dungeon Master narrates exactly what happens to
    your character based on the number that landed. Your current HP is always
    visible in the terminal header so you can track your survival at a glance.

    You are now playing. Type your next action to continue the adventure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep Playing

After your first roll the loop is the same: read the narration, decide what your character does next, type it, roll, repeat. Work through the dungeon, defeat the henchman, find the boss, survive. If you reach 0 HP your run ends — but your chronicle is saved and you can export it as Markdown before starting a new character.
