Two enemy pools stock Terminal 1d6 RPG's encounters — henchmen (weaker foes that appear early and frequently) and bosses (powerful adversaries that emerge on high encounter-shift rolls). Understanding which pool your next enemy is drawn from can shape how you narrate each confrontation in your story log.

## When Each Enemy Type Appears

- **Henchmen**: Your starting enemy is always drawn from the henchmen pool. During subsequent encounter shifts, a secondary d6 roll of **1–4** also draws from the henchmen pool.
- **Bosses**: During an encounter shift, a secondary d6 roll of **5–6** draws from the boss pool, pitting you against one of the game's most fearsome adversaries.
