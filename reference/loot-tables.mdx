The loot pool contains 25 unique treasure items. One is drawn at the start of your campaign (guarded by your first enemy), and additional items are drawn whenever you roll a 6 (Perfect Triumph). Each drawn item is recorded in your story log and included in your exported chronicle, building a tangible record of everything you have claimed in your run.

<Note>
Loot items are narrative flavor — they appear in the story log and your exported chronicle but do not grant mechanical HP or stat bonuses. Think of your growing collection as a testament to your lucky rolls, not a power-up system.
</Note>

<Info>
Once an item is drawn, it cannot appear again in the same run. With 25 items in the pool, a long campaign may eventually exhaust all available treasure. When the pool runs dry, the game continues — you simply will not receive a new item name on subsequent Perfect Triumph rolls.
</Info>
