Every encounter shift transports you to a new location drawn from the 25-entry location pool. Your starting location is also drawn from this pool at the beginning of the campaign, grounding your first scene in one of these 25 distinct dungeon settings. Each new location is recorded in your story log, charting the full geography of your run.
