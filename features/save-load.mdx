Terminal 1d6 RPG saves your progress automatically — you never need to open a menu or press a save button. Every action you take, every die you roll, and every piece of loot you claim is written to your browser's `localStorage` the moment it happens. Close the tab, shut the laptop, walk away — your adventure will be exactly where you left it the next time you return.

## What Is Saved

All data is stored under the localStorage key `terminal_1d6_rpg_state`. The following fields are preserved in full after every single action:

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `character` | All 9 wizard fields (name, class, timeline, familiar, bio, weapons, strengths, struggles, notes) plus current and max HP |
| `logs` | Array of every terminal message — the entire scroll history, every line of DM output and your commands |
| `tables` | Remaining pool entries for all six procedural tables, so draws continue from where they stopped |
| `inCombat` | Boolean — whether you are currently in a fight |
| `currentEnemy` | The name of the current enemy, if any |
| `gameOver` | Boolean — whether your run has concluded |
| `ttsEnabled` | Whether narration was enabled or disabled |
| `wizardStep` | Which step of the character-creation wizard you had reached |

## How Session Restore Works

When you open the game and a saved session is found in `terminal_1d6_rpg_state`, the full action log is replayed into the terminal to reconstruct your screen exactly as you left it. Once the replay completes, the following system message appears in the terminal:

```
[SYSTEM] Previous adventure session restored from local storage.
```

Your character sheet repopulates, your pool entries reload, and combat resumes if you were mid-fight. You can continue playing immediately.

<Warning>
  Saves are stored in the browser on the device you're playing on. Clearing your browser data or playing in a private or incognito window will erase your save permanently.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Save data is never sent to any server — it lives entirely on your device.
</Note>

## Resetting Your Campaign

If you want to start fresh, click the **Reset** button in the top-right corner of the toolbar (marked in red). The game will prompt you to confirm before taking any action. Once you confirm:

1. Your `localStorage` save is cleared completely.
2. The page reloads and launches a brand-new campaign from the character creation wizard.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

<Tip>
  Before resetting, use **Export JSON** or **Export Story** to preserve a record of your adventure. See the [Export](/features/export) page for details.
</Tip>
