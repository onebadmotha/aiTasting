Terminal 1d6 RPG includes a built-in text-to-speech (TTS) narration feature that reads every Dungeon Master message aloud using your browser's native speech synthesis API. No plugins, no downloads, and no internet connection required — the voice lives entirely inside your browser, making it available even when you're playing offline.

## Toggling TTS On and Off

You control narration from the top toolbar with a single button:

- Click the **TTS: ON / TTS: OFF** button to toggle narration at any time during your session.
- When TTS is **ON**, the button is highlighted in **cyan** so you can confirm the setting at a glance.
- When TTS is **OFF**, the button grays out and narration stops immediately.
- Your preference is saved automatically alongside your game state — when you reload the page and restore a session, TTS picks up exactly as you left it.

## What Gets Narrated

Every piece of Dungeon Master output is read aloud, including:

- Encounter opening and closing text
- Dice roll outcome descriptions
- Calamity and triumph event passages (bad and good event draws)
- Epilogue text at the end of your run

Your own typed commands and inputs are **not** narrated — only the DM's responses are spoken.

## TTS Speech Settings

The narration uses the following browser-level speech parameters to give the voice a measured, atmospheric quality:

- **Speech rate:** `0.95` — slightly slower than the browser default, giving each word room to land.
- **Pitch:** `0.9` — slightly lower than default, lending the DM's voice a grounded, authoritative tone.

## Automatic Symbol Stripping

Markdown and formatting symbols are automatically removed from the text before it is sent to the speech engine. Characters including `* # _ [ ] >` are stripped out so they never interrupt the flow of narration or produce awkward spoken artefacts.

## Browser Support

<Note>
  TTS uses the Web Speech API built into your browser — no external service or internet connection is needed.
</Note>

| Browser | TTS Support |
|---------|-------------|
| Chrome | ✅ Full support |
| Edge | ✅ Full support |
| Safari | ✅ Full support |
| Firefox | ✅ Supported (may vary by OS voice pack) |

<Tip>
  If TTS cuts off mid-sentence, try switching to Chrome or Edge, which have the most reliable speech synthesis implementations.
</Tip>
