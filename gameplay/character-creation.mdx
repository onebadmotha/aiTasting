---
title: "Build Your Hero in Terminal 1d6 RPG Character Creation"
sidebarTitle: "Character Creation"
description: "Walk through the nine-question character creation wizard in Terminal 1d6 RPG and learn how your answers shape the Dungeon Master's storytelling."
---

When you launch Terminal 1d6 RPG for the first time — or after a full reset — the character creation wizard starts automatically in the terminal. It asks you nine questions, one at a time, and uses every answer to shape how the Dungeon Master narrates your story. There are no stat blocks, no skill trees, and no menus: just your words, typed directly into the terminal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name">
    Type your adventurer's name. This is how the Dungeon Master will address you throughout the campaign. Choose something that fits the world you want to inhabit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Class">
    Enter your character's class. The game accepts any free-text class you invent — you are not locked to a preset list. Examples include **Rogue**, **Chrono-Mage**, and **Oathbreaker Paladin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timeline / Era">
    Describe the era or setting of your story. This anchors the Dungeon Master's tone and the flavor of the locations, enemies, and loot it generates. Examples: *The Age of Ashen Rust*, *Post-Collapse Year 442*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Familiar / Companion">
    Name a companion and provide their species, or type `None` if you travel alone. Your familiar appears in the Character Chronicle and colors how the DM frames events around you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Background">
    Write a brief paragraph about who your character is — their history, motivations, and place in the world. The richer this entry, the more the DM has to draw from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weapons & Tools">
    List what you carry into battle: weapons, tools, and any special equipment. These items inform the DM's descriptions of your combat actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strengths">
    Name two things your character is genuinely good at. The DM weaves these into successful outcomes and positive-progress narration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Struggles">
    Name two things your character finds hard. These show up in complications, calamities, and the flavor of a bad roll.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DM Notes">
    Add anything else the Dungeon Master should know — secrets, goals, grudges, or quirks that don't fit the earlier questions. This is your direct line to the narrator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you answer all nine questions, the game compiles your entries and displays your **Character Chronicle** — a summary of every field alongside your starting stats. Your chronicle also includes a starting HP value of **10/10**, a procedurally drawn starting location, a starting henchman enemy you must face immediately, and a starting loot item waiting to be claimed.

```
==============================================
CHARACTER CHRONICLE ACCEPTED
==============================================
Name:      Kael Duskbane
Class:     Shadow Rogue
Timeline:  The Age of Ashen Rust
Familiar:  Nixie (Shadow Cat)
Armament:  Twin daggers, smoke bombs
Strengths: Stealthy, quick reflexes
Burden:    Haunted past, distrusts magic
Notes:     Seeks the artifact of the Ashen Court
Vitality:  10 / 10 HP
==============================================

You materialize into: [Sunken Crypt of the Weeping Martyr].
Before you stands a scout: [Shadow-Bound Rogue] guarding [Runed Dagger of Soul-Siphon].
```

<Tip>
  The more vivid and specific your answers, the richer the DM's narration will be. A class of "Shadow Rogue" and a timeline of "The Age of Ashen Rust" will produce far more evocative prose than "Fighter" and "Medieval."
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your character data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. If you close and reopen the game, your session resumes exactly where you left off — no manual save required.
</Note>
