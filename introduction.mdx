Terminal 1d6 RPG is a solo text-based dungeon crawler that runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no installs, no accounts. You play an adventurer navigating procedurally generated dungeons where every action, from swinging a sword to picking a lock, is resolved by rolling a single virtual six-sided die. The game saves your progress automatically and works fully offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA), so you can carry your adventure on any device, anywhere.

<Note>
  No account or sign-up is required. Open the game URL and you are ready to
  play. Your session is saved locally in your browser and never sent to any
  server.
</Note>

## What You Get

Every run generates a fresh dungeon populated with unique enemies, locations, and loot drawn from large procedural pools. The terminal-style interface puts you directly in conversation with a virtual Dungeon Master that narrates every outcome and reacts to your free-form actions.

## How the Die Works

You type a free-form action — anything from "Attack the henchman with my axe" to "Try to bribe the guard" — and the game rolls a d6 on your behalf. The number that lands determines how well your action succeeds or fails:

| Roll | Outcome            | What It Means                                      |
| ---- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 6    | Perfect Triumph    | Your action succeeds completely, with a bonus.     |
| 5    | Strong Success     | Your action succeeds cleanly.                      |
| 4    | Partial Success    | You succeed, but with a minor complication.        |
| 3    | Mixed Outcome      | Success and setback arrive together.               |
| 2    | Setback            | Your action fails or backfires in a meaningful way.|
| 1    | Critical Calamity  | The worst possible result — and you lose 1 HP.     |

The Dungeon Master narrates the outcome in the terminal, weaving the die result into the story so every roll feels consequential.
